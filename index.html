Other Activities

These are possibilities for the open days. No dates are assigned; the idea is to choose from them during the week rather than lock the family into a schedule.

🌌Universal Epic Universe — Nintendo, How to Train Your Dragon, Harry Potter, and large themed lands built for exploring. Likely the busiest park option, but it directly matches Elanor's strongest interests.
🦁Disney's Animal Kingdom — Animals, safari, Pandora, trails, and shows with the easiest pace for the whole group. Strongest all-around choice even if nobody wants to chase rides all day.
🐊Airboat Adventure — Recommended Kissimmee-area airboat ride; operator and ride length can be chosen later.
⚔️Medieval Times dinner show — A possible evening activity in Kissimmee.
🚗Scenic drive / nature walk — Choose an optional scenic drive to Winter Haven or a nature walk.
🏊Pool, house & family time — Keep an open day at the villa, have casual meals, and spend time with C2.
Other Parks Considered
⭐ Disney's Hollywood Studios
Star Wars, Toy Story, shows, and movie-themed attractions with several non-coaster options. Best match for Lisa's household; Galaxy's Edge is the main reason to include it.
🌍 Universal Islands of Adventure
Jurassic Park, Hogsmeade, Marvel, and Seuss; a strong ride-heavy alternative.
🏰 Magic Kingdom
Classic Disney atmosphere, familiar attractions, parade, castle, and fireworks.
🎬 Universal Studios Florida
Diagon Alley, Minions, DreamWorks, and movie-based indoor attractions.
🌐 EPCOT
World Showcase, food, festivals, aquarium, and a slower adult-oriented touring style.
