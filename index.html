The Trip

This fall, Grand/Dad is retiring, and the family is getting together in Orlando for a week built first around visiting Cathy and Charlie near Kissimmee. Disney World, Universal Studios, and a few other Orlando-area attractions are part of the fun, but the center of the trip is time together as family.

The week is planned around staying together near Kissimmee, making it easy to visit with Cathy and Charlie, enjoy the parks, share meals, hang out at the rented house, and keep the schedule full without turning it into a forced march.

These are tentative plans and working estimates as of mid-May 2026. Details will change as flights, lodging, park days, and activity reservations are finalized.
